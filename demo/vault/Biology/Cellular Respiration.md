Cellular respiration turns glucose into usable [[Energy]] (ATP) inside the [[Mitochondria]].

$$
C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6\,O_2 \rightarrow 6\,CO_2 + 6\,H_2O + \text{energy}
$$

Stages of cellular respiration >>
1. Glycolysis (cytoplasm)
2. Citric acid cycle (mitochondrial matrix)
3. Electron transport chain (inner membrane)

Net ATP per glucose (approx.) >> About 30–32 ATP

It is roughly the reverse of [[Photosynthesis]].
