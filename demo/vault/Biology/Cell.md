The **cell** is the smallest unit of life. Every living organism is made of one or more cells.

Smallest unit of life >> The cell

Two main types of cells >>
- Prokaryotic (no nucleus, e.g. bacteria)
- Eukaryotic (with nucleus, e.g. plants, animals)

Important organelles: [[Nucleus]], [[Mitochondria]], ribosomes and the cell membrane.
