The French Revolution (1789–1799) ended the absolute monarchy in France.

When did the storming of the Bastille happen? >> 14 July 1789

Motto of the revolution >> Liberté, égalité, fraternité

Key phases >>
- Estates-General and National Assembly (1789)
- Constitutional monarchy (1791)
- The Terror (1793–1794)
- The Directory (1795–1799)
